TPKX Map Factory is a map-manufacturing and format-conversion workflow. It can render or package map content from sources configured in QGIS, but the software does not grant rights to imagery, labels, basemaps, or other source data.

Users are responsible for complying with the terms, licenses, caching rules, export restrictions, attribution requirements, and redistribution rules that apply to each source they use.

The technical capability to request, render, cache, package, or display content should not be interpreted as a statement about legal rights to redistribute that content.

The custom MBTiles -> TPKX converter itself operates on already-created raster MBTiles and performs deterministic packaging/address conversion into the Esri Compact Cache V2 / TPKX structure.
